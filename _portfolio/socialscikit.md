**socialscikit** lowers the technical barrier for computational social science by packaging common NLP workflows — text classification, sentiment analysis, topic modeling, and LLM-assisted annotation — behind a simple, no-code interface. Built for researchers in psychology, political science, and communication who want to analyze large text corpora without deep programming expertise.

- **Repository:** [github.com/Baron-Sun/socialscikit](https://github.com/Baron-Sun/socialscikit)
