<section class="home-section home-bio" aria-labelledby="about-heading">
  <header class="section-heading">
    <h2 id="about-heading">About</h2>
  </header>
  <div class="home-section__body">
    <p>I am a Ph.D. student at the <a href="https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/">Kellogg School of Management</a>, <a href="https://www.northwestern.edu/">Northwestern University</a>, working with Professor <a href="https://williamjbrady.com/">William J. Brady</a>.</p>
    <p>Before joining Northwestern, I was a Research Engineer at the <a href="https://snfagora.jhu.edu/">SNF Agora Institute at Johns Hopkins University</a>, working with Professor <a href="https://politicalscience.jhu.edu/directory/yuen-yuen-ang/">Yuen Yuen Ang</a> and <a href="https://charleschang.me/">Charles Chang</a>. I hold dual master’s degrees in Information Systems and Computer Science from Johns Hopkins, with a concentration in Human Language Technology.</p>
    <p>I earned my bachelor’s degree in Informatics from the <a href="https://ics.uci.edu/">Donald Bren School of Information and Computer Sciences at UC Irvine</a>, specializing in Human-Computer Interaction and Health Informatics, and worked with Professor <a href="https://markwarschauer.com/">Mark Warschauer</a> and Dr. Dandan Yang on educational technology. My research is computational and interdisciplinary, combining AI and NLP methods with perspectives from computational social science, HCI, and behavioral science. I am also increasingly incorporating behavioral experiments from the social sciences to study how people interact with and make decisions alongside AI. I have published multiple papers in top-tier conferences, including ICLR, NeurIPS, and ACL, where I also serve as a reviewer.</p>
